01 — Our coffee

Badger & Dodo, roasted in Cork, pulled here on The Plaza.

Our USP is simple: we love coffee, we’re serious about coffee, and we’ve sourced the best beans to make it. Ask at the counter and we’ll happily talk you through what’s on the grinder — what it tastes like, where it came from, and how we’d drink it.

Tea drinkers are looked after too, with loose leaf from Suki Tea in Belfast.

02 — Small on purpose

There are bigger places to get a coffee in Sandyford. That’s rather the point.

WE KNOW YOUR ORDER

Come in twice and we’ll have started it before you reach the counter. Come in a third time and we’ll ask how the interview went.

ONE GRINDER, DONE WELL

No twenty-item syrup wall. A short menu, dialled in every morning, and a barista who can tell you exactly what’s in the hopper.

IN AND OUT IN THREE

Most of the Quarter is on a lunch break. We keep the line moving without rushing the shot, and there’s seating outside when you have the time.

We’d rather buy from people we can name than from a catalogue. Every element in the café has been sourced with thought and care — the beans, the tea, the bread, the treats, and yes, the crisps.
